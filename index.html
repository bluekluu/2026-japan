Location-specific precautions

Tokyo · Oct 31–Nov 4
Earthquakes and transport/utility interruptions

Use the Tokyo Arrival Checklist for the first hotel. Check emergency stairs, keep a grab pouch, and note your assembly point.

Karuizawa · Nov 4–7
Cold weather and Mt. Asama volcanic restrictions

Bring warm layers, rain protection, snacks and a charged phone. Check local weather and any volcanic access restrictions before mountain outings.

Toyama · Nov 7–11
Mountain weather, landslides and railway disruptions

Check Kurobe Gorge Railway status on the day of travel. Carry water, snacks, warm layers and allow a backup sightseeing plan.

Takayama · Nov 11–14
Cold mountain conditions and possible transit delays

Wear grippy shoes and warm layers. Check train and bus operations and keep flexibility for onward travel.

Kumamoto · Nov 14–17
Earthquakes and Mt. Aso volcanic activity

Before visiting Aso, check the current volcanic alert level and official crater-access restrictions. Never enter a restricted area.

Fukuoka / Hakata · Nov 17–20
Earthquakes and coastal tsunami exposure

When visiting waterfront areas, identify a route to higher ground or a designated tsunami evacuation building. After strong or long shaking near the coast, evacuate immediately.

Emergency actions & links

During shaking: Drop, cover, hold on. Avoid windows and tall furniture. Do not use elevators after shaking. Follow hotel staff and official instructions.

At the coast: Following strong or prolonged shaking, move promptly to high ground or an evacuation building; don't wait for an official tsunami alert.

Japan Visitor Hotline: 050-3816-2787 (international format +81 50-3816-2787).
